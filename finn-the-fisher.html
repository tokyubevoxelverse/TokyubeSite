Finn the Fisher

Tokyube Presents: Finn the Fisher — an ocean adventure that's a whole standalone game inside Tokyube. Dive the deep for fish, dodge the jellyfish, haul your catch back to the boat, build your seafood empire — and between dives, go bird hunting or draw your own sledding tracks.

▶ PLAY NOW — 5 MINUTES FREE IN YOUR BROWSER

Overview

Finn the Fisher is the second standalone game in the Discovery Portal (after Battle Buddies Magic Quest). Its world page wears the classic flamingo-pink borders, and PLAY launches the game directly — complete with its own splash intro that fades from the world art into the Finn logo before the title screen.

Launching & Returning

  1. Open DISCOVER NEW WORLDS from the main menu.
  2. Click the Finn the Fisher featured card.
  3. Hit ▶ PLAY — the splash sequence plays, then you're on Finn's title screen.
  4. The ocean-styled ◀ BACK TO TOKYUBE button (top-left, title screen only) returns you straight to the VoxelVerse title — no launch splash replay.

Diving & Fishing

The core loop: dive from the boat, fill your bag with fish, and surface before you black out. Jellyfish sting, the deep holds the rare stuff, and an escape pod can rocket you back to the boat when a dive goes sideways. Black out underwater and a deckhand hauls you in — you keep your catch, but pride is another matter.

The Boat, Shop & Empire

Between dives, the boat is home base: sell the catch, chase the daily goal, pour and serve for customers, stock the vending machine, buy upgrades and unlock skins in the shop. Money carries day to day as your seafood empire grows — nights bring their own moods, and each of the five save slots is its own career.

Mini Games

Finn packs two full mini games, each with its own saves, currencies and unlocks — they're games within the game within the game.

Bird Hunt

An archery range in the sky:

  • Bows & arrows — start with the wood bow and classic arrows; unlock more, plus speed and knockback upgrades.
  • Lands — begin in the meadow and shoot your way to new hunting grounds, up to the volcano where only phoenixes fly — fast as comets, worth ✦10 a bird.
  • Birds — a whole flock of species with different speeds and sizes (good luck hitting the hummingbird).
  • Streaks — multi-hit streaks pay coin bonuses; coins buy the next bow, arrow and land.

Line Sledding

The line-rider mini game — draw the track, ride the track:

  • Draw your own lines and send the sled down them; scroll to zoom the camera in and out.
  • Three line types — normal, accelerator and slow lines, each with its own color you can retheme.
  • Sled colors — a 17-color palette from Black and HotPink to Mint and Deep.
  • Per-slot saves — your tracks and sled setup persist per save slot.

Always the Newest Build
