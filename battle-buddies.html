Battle Buddies Magic Quest

A whole game inside Tokyube — grab your battle buddies, pick a starting character and set off on a magic quest of spells, monsters and loot. Battle Buddies is the rainbow crown of the Discovery Portal: it looks like a featured world, but PLAY launches a complete standalone adventure.

Overview

Battle Buddies Magic Quest is not a build world. It's its own game — its own engine scene, its own menus, its own save slots — that ships inside the Tokyube VoxelVerse and launches straight from the Discovery Portal. Expect creature-collecting adventure: starting characters, elemental buddies, wild clearings, dungeons and loot, all wrapped in a flowing lava-lamp aesthetic.

Launching & Returning

  1. Open DISCOVER NEW WORLDS from the main menu.
  2. Click the Battle Buddies Magic Quest card — the one with the animated rainbow border.
  3. On its rainbow world page, hit ▶ PLAY. The game launches directly — no world install, no download screen.
  4. To come back, use the rainbow ◀ BACK TO TOKYUBE button pinned to the top-left of the game — it returns you to the VoxelVerse title screen.

Save Slots

Battle Buddies has five save slots, separate from your Tokyube worlds. Each slot is its own adventure — pick a slot from the title screen to continue or start fresh.

Characters & Buddies

  • Starting characters — choose your hero at the start of a new save.
  • Battle buddies — the creatures that fight at your side. Buddies come in elemental types, and your buddy has your back: if you go down, a buddy can shield you back to your feet — at a price.
  • Buddy Balls — the item you'll be throwing to grow your team.

Exploring & Dungeons

The world alternates safe ground with danger. Event banners warn you as you cross thresholds — "You've entered a dangerous clearing. Be careful!" — and dungeons branch into multiple exits, so keep your bearings. Kills during a run count toward your exit purse, so fighting deeper pays out when you make it back.

Combat

Fights play out with your buddies against wild monsters, with a battle log tracking the three most recent actions so you always know what just hit you. Damage flashes the screen; loot and experience flow from victories into your growing quest.

Alpha Status

Battle Buddies Magic Quest is in alpha test — it shipped with VoxelVerse v0.0.138 as the Discovery Portal's featured rainbow world. Content is expanding build by build; expect new buddies, zones and quest content in the patch notes. Found something broken? File a bug and mention Battle Buddies.
