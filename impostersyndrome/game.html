HOW TO PLAY

Crewmates win by finishing every task on the ship, or by voting out all Impostors. Impostors win by killing until they equal the crew, or by letting a Reactor/O2 sabotage run out.

Move with WASD/arrows or the touch stick. E or USE opens tasks, panels, the Emergency Button, Admin table and Security cams. R reports bodies. M/Tab opens the map. Esc pauses.

Impostors: Q kills nearby crew, F uses vents, the map doubles as a sabotage console (lights, reactor, O2, comms, doors). Fake tasks to blend in.

Meetings: a report or the Emergency Button freezes the round. Bots genuinely remember what they saw — who they were with, who they saw vent or kill. Talk with the quick-chat buttons, then vote. Ties and skips eject no one.

Ghosts keep doing tasks (or haunting) after death. The bar at top-left tracks total crew task progress.
