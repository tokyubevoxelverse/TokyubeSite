OuterVerse

OuterVerse: A Space Exploration Sandbox prototyped fully with in-engine Tokyube tools. An infinite galaxy engine — launch from planet to planet, mine, build and roam a procedural universe that never ends. The fourth standalone game inside the Tokyube VoxelVerse.

Overview

OuterVerse is a whole space game living inside Tokyube: a procedural galaxy where every star is somewhere you can actually go. It launches straight from the Discovery Portal — no world install — and like every standalone title, the engine always runs the newest build automatically.

Play Free in Browser

You can try OuterVerse right now at tokyube.com/outerverse — five free minutes in your browser. When the timer's up: enjoying? You can keep playing and load your progress in Tokyube on Steam.

Launching & Returning

  1. Open DISCOVER NEW WORLDS from the main menu.
  2. Scroll to Official TOKYUBE Worlds and click OuterVerse.
  3. Hit ▶ PLAY — the game launches directly.
  4. The cosmic ◀ BACK TO TOKYUBE button (top-left) returns you to the VoxelVerse title screen — no splash replay, and it scales with your Options display-resolution setting.

The Infinite Galaxy

The universe is procedural and unending: fly between planets, drop into atmosphere, and touch down on worlds that generate as you arrive. Every planet is explorable terrain, not a skybox.

Mining & Building

OuterVerse is a sandbox: mine what you find and build where you stand — press B to build and Tab for the menu when prompted on a planet's surface.

The Exosuit Forge

Starting a NEW FILE opens the Exosuit Forge — OuterVerse's procedural character creator. Forge your suit, and the game brings you straight back to your new save with the suit equipped. Launched from Tokyube, the round trip keeps your Back-to-Tokyube button intact. (The Forge streams its renderer from the web, so character creation needs an internet connection.)

Graphics Options

The title screen's OPTIONS panel is a full graphics suite. Pick a preset — OPTIMIZED, LOW, MEDIUM, HIGH, ULTRA — or tune any switch yourself (which flips the preset to CUSTOM). Everything persists between sessions:

  • Display Resolution — AUTO / NATIVE / 1440P / 1200P / 1080P / 900P / 720P (the label shows the true pixel dimensions).
  • Render Distance — NEAR / STANDARD / FAR / ULTRA, and Render Scale — 75% / 100% / 125%.
  • FPS Cap — UNCAPPED / 30 / 45 / 60 / 90 / 120 — plus VSync.
  • Quality toggles — Anti-Alias, Shadows, Bloom, Motion Blur, and a four-step Vignette.
  • World density — Grass Density (LOW→ULTRA), Foliage Density (LOW→HIGH, regrows the title flora live), and Leaf Fall FX.
  • Controls & audio — Invert Y, plus SFX / Ambient-Ship-Hum / Music volume sliders.

Built with Tokyube Tools

OuterVerse was prototyped fully with in-engine Tokyube tools — the same three.js foundation, the same iteration loop the VoxelVerse itself is built on. It's the proof-of-concept for what a solo dev can ship inside the Tokyube ecosystem: see the showcase video on the homepage.
