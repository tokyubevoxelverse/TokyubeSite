Jumpin' Ain't Easy!

Jumpin' Ain't Easy! is a comic-ink vertical scramble where every jump is earned — climb, bounce, shop upgrades and chase the leaderboard. A standalone game inside Tokyube with a trippy RGB world page to match its attitude.

Overview

Thick ink outlines, chunky buttons and a dark-navy stage: JAE is a climb-'em-up where gravity holds a grudge. It lives in Official TOKYUBE Worlds and always runs its newest build automatically.

Launching & Returning

  1. Open DISCOVER NEW WORLDS from the main menu.
  2. Scroll to Official TOKYUBE Worlds and click Jumpin' Ain't Easy!.
  3. On its trippy RGB world page, hit ▶ PLAY.
  4. The comic-ink ◀ BACK TO TOKYUBE button (top-left) appears on the game's title screen and returns you to the VoxelVerse — no splash replay, scaled to your Options display resolution.

How It Plays

  • Climb — every jump is a commitment; misjudge it and the fall is all yours.
  • The shop sells upgrades between runs.
  • The board keeps score — your best climb is always waiting to be beaten.

Pausing

JAE has its own native pause: press ESC mid-run (or the pause button on the HUD) to freeze; the game also auto-pauses if the window loses focus. Quit-to-title lives on the pause screen — where the Back-to-Tokyube button will be waiting.

The Trippy RGB World Page

The discovery page is a mood: a dark navy stage washed by a continuously hue-cycling RGB sweep, the game's rainbow logo gradient on the title, and an ink-outlined PLAY button. Likes, comments and visits work like every other world page.

Status
