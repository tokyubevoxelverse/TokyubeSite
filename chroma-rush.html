Chroma Rush

CHROMA RUSH is a neon color-dash arcade game under a night sky — a standalone title inside Tokyube. One tap to play, impossible to put down: ride the rainbow, chain the chroma, and chase your high score.

Overview

Chroma Rush is the third standalone game inside the Tokyube VoxelVerse (after Battle Buddies Magic Quest and Finn the Fisher). It's pure arcade: instant restarts, an ever-climbing pace, and a scoreboard that taunts you into one more run.

Launching & Returning

  1. Open DISCOVER NEW WORLDS from the main menu.
  2. Scroll to Official TOKYUBE Worlds and click Chroma Rush.
  3. On its black-and-rainbow world page, hit ▶ PLAY — the game launches directly, no world install.
  4. From the game's menu, the rainbow-ringed ◀ BACK TO TOKYUBE button (top-left) returns you to the VoxelVerse title screen — no splash replay, and the button scales with your Options display-resolution setting.

How It Plays

  • Menu → Play → Dead → again. The whole game is one loop, and the loop is the point.
  • Dash through the neon field, match and chain colors, and keep your run alive as the speed ramps.
  • Death is instant and so is the retry — your best run is always on screen, daring you.
  • ESC pauses for real. The game freezes mid-run and a Chroma-styled pause menu offers Resume, Restart, and Back to Tokyube. The rainbow-ringed ◀ BACK TO TOKYUBE corner button is also always available.

The Night-Sky World Page

Chroma Rush's discovery page has its own identity: a pure black night sky full of twinkling stars, with rainbow accents — a rainbow-bordered thumbnail, a rainbow-gradient title, and an animated rainbow PLAY button. Likes, comments and visit counts work like every other world page.

Status

Chroma Rush shipped with VoxelVerse v0.0.142 in Official TOKYUBE Worlds. Like every standalone title, the engine always ships the newest build of the game automatically. Found something broken? File a bug and mention Chroma Rush.
