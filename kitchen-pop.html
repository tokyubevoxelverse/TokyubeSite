KITCHEN POP!

KITCHEN POP! is a candy-bright kitchen arcade — pop, match and serve your way through sugar-rush minigames. A standalone game inside Tokyube: pick it up in seconds, put it down never.

Overview

Kitchen Pop is pure dessert: bubbly menus, chunky candy buttons and quick rounds built for one-more-go energy. It lives in Official TOKYUBE Worlds and launches straight from its world page — no world install, and the engine always runs the newest build automatically.

Launching & Returning

  1. Open DISCOVER NEW WORLDS from the main menu.
  2. Scroll to Official TOKYUBE Worlds and click KITCHEN POP!.
  3. On its pink-lemonade world page, hit ▶ PLAY.
  4. The candy-pink ◀ BACK TO TOKYUBE pill (top-left) appears on the game's title screen and returns you to the VoxelVerse — no splash replay, and it scales with your Options display-resolution setting.

How It Plays

  • Pick your dish from the select screen and dive into the minigame.
  • One-button action — SPACE (or a click) drives the popping and serving.
  • Results screens tally your sugar rush; the ✕ button hops you back to the menu for another round.

The Pink Lemonade World Page

Kitchen Pop's discovery page is pure pink lemonade — a peach → pink → lemon gradient with candy-pink buttons and dark-berry text. Likes, comments and visit counts work like every other world page.

Status

KITCHEN POP! shipped with VoxelVerse v0.0.145 in Official TOKYUBE Worlds. Found something broken? File a bug and mention Kitchen Pop.
