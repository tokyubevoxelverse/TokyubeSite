Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy explains how DOOM Industries, LLC (“DOOM Industries,” “we,” “us,” or “our”) collects, uses, shares, and protects information in connection with TokyubeVoxelVerse (the “Game”), the website at tokyube.com, and related services (together, the “Services”).

Effective date: June 16, 2026  ·  Last updated: June 16, 2026

Introduction

TokyubeVoxelVerse is a multiplayer voxel sandbox and creation platform distributed through Steam and available worldwide. By creating an account, signing in through a third-party platform, or otherwise using the Services, you agree to the practices described in this Policy. If you do not agree, please do not use the Services.

We try to collect only what we need to run the Game, sync your account across devices, enable multiplayer, and process purchases.

Information We Collect

Account & sign-in information

You can sign in using third-party platforms. When you do, we receive a limited profile from that provider — typically an account identifier, a username/display name, and (where provided) an avatar image. Supported sign-in providers include:

  • Steam — Steam ID, profile name, avatar.
  • Epic Games — Epic account ID and display name (Epic Account Services).
  • Discord — user ID, username, avatar.
  • Google — account ID, name, email (where you grant it).
  • Microsoft / Xbox — account identifier and gamertag.
  • YouTube/Google sign-in — used optionally to enable signed-in video playback on in-world screens.

We do not receive your third-party password. The exact data shared is governed by that provider and by the permissions you approve.

Profile & creations

Information you create in the Services, such as your in-game username, profile picture, custom avatars (“Tokytars”), worlds, code-block scripts, components, and other user-generated content that you choose to save to your account.

Purchases & virtual currency

Records related to your purchase and use of in-game currency (“TokyTokinz” / TTZ) and other digital items — for example, transaction identifiers, the items or currency granted, your balance, and entitlement (what you own). Payment card details are handled by the platform store (e.g., Steam); we do not collect or store your full payment card information.

Multiplayer & voice

When you play multiplayer, your account identifier and in-session presence are shared with other participants and with the session host to enable play. If you use in-game voice chat, your audio is transmitted to other players in your session in real time.

Technical & usage information

Information generated automatically when you use the Services, such as IP address, general device and connection information, in-game session and crash/diagnostic logs, and gameplay events used to operate and improve the Game. We may use aggregated or de-identified analytics to understand feature usage.

How We Use Information

  • Create and manage your account and sign you in.
  • Sync your profile, avatars, creations, balances, and entitlements across devices.
  • Provide multiplayer matchmaking, sessions, and voice chat.
  • Process and deliver in-app purchases and virtual currency, and prevent fraudulent or duplicate transactions.
  • Operate, maintain, debug, secure, and improve the Services.
  • Enforce our terms, prevent abuse and cheating, and keep the community safe.
  • Communicate with you about support requests and important service notices.
  • Comply with legal obligations.

How We Share Information

We do not sell your personal information. We share information only as described here:

  • Service providers. We use trusted vendors to run the Services, including Supabase (account database, authentication, and storage hosting) and platform providers listed above. They process data on our behalf under agreements that limit their use of it.
  • Platform & storefront partners. Steam (and, where applicable, Epic) process purchases and provide core platform features such as identity and peer-to-peer networking. Their handling of your data is governed by their own privacy policies.
  • Other players. Your username, avatar, presence, and voice are visible/audible to others in shared multiplayer sessions, and your account ID is shared with peers and hosts to enable play.
  • Legal & safety. When required by law, or to protect the rights, safety, and property of DOOM Industries, our users, or the public.
  • Business transfers. In connection with a merger, acquisition, financing, or sale of assets, information may be transferred as part of that transaction.

Payments & In-App Purchases

The Game offers optional purchases of virtual currency (TokyTokinz) and digital items. Purchases are processed through the platform store you bought the Game on (for example, the Steam microtransaction system). The store handles your payment method; we receive a confirmation and the resulting entitlement so we can grant your currency or item.

Virtual currency and digital items have no monetary value, are not redeemable for cash, and are licensed — not sold — to you for use within the Services, except where required by law. Refund eligibility is governed by the applicable store's policies.

Multiplayer, Voice & User Content

Content you choose to share — worlds, scripts, avatars, names, and voice chat — may be seen or heard by other players. Please do not share information in public or shared spaces that you want to keep private. We are not responsible for information you voluntarily disclose to other players. Voice chat is transmitted to other participants for live communication; we do not retain recordings of voice chat for ordinary gameplay.

Cookies & Similar Technologies

The website and the Game's embedded web views use cookies and similar local storage to keep you signed in, remember your settings, and operate features (for example, optional YouTube playback on in-world screens). You can control cookies through your browser or device settings, though some features may not work without them.

Data Storage & Security

Account data is stored with our hosting provider (Supabase), and much of your gameplay data (worlds, local saves) lives on your own device. We use reasonable administrative, technical, and organizational safeguards to protect information. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

Data Retention

We keep personal information for as long as your account is active or as needed to provide the Services, comply with our legal obligations, resolve disputes, prevent fraud, and enforce our agreements. When information is no longer needed, we delete or de-identify it. You may request deletion of your account data as described below.

Your Privacy Rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights over your personal information, including the right to access, correct, delete, or receive a copy of it, and to object to or restrict certain processing.

  • EEA/UK (GDPR): rights of access, rectification, erasure, restriction, portability, and objection. Our legal bases include performance of a contract (running the Game), legitimate interests (security, improvement), consent (where requested), and legal compliance. You may also lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority.
  • California (CCPA/CPRA): rights to know, access, correct, and delete personal information, and to opt out of “sale” or “sharing” of personal information. We do not sell or share personal information as those terms are defined under California law.

To exercise any of these rights, contact us at tokyubevoxelverse@gmail.com. We will verify your request and respond within the time required by applicable law. We will not discriminate against you for exercising your rights.

Children's Privacy

The Services are not directed to children under 13 (or the minimum age required in your country), and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has provided us personal information, contact us at tokyubevoxelverse@gmail.com and we will take steps to delete it. Where required, parents or guardians may contact us to review or remove their child's information.

International Users

TokyubeVoxelVerse is available worldwide. Your information may be processed and stored in the United States or other countries where we or our service providers operate, which may have different data protection laws than your own. Where required, we use appropriate safeguards for international transfers. By using the Services, you understand your information may be transferred to these locations.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Policy from time to time. When we make material changes, we will update the “Last updated” date above and, where appropriate, provide additional notice. Your continued use of the Services after an update means you accept the revised Policy.
