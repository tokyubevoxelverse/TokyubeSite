BerryBlendz

BerryBlendz — drop, stack and blend the berries. A juicy merge arcade with shops, scoreboards and combos that pop, living inside Tokyube under a berry-dark world page: deep blue into indigo, purple, hot pink and red.

Overview

Aim, drop, merge: matching berries blend into bigger, juicier ones, and the combos snowball. Rounded candy UI, a shop, high-score boards and a how-to screen round it out. It lives in Official TOKYUBE Worlds and always runs its newest build automatically.

Launching & Returning

  1. Open DISCOVER NEW WORLDS from the main menu.
  2. Scroll to Official TOKYUBE Worlds and click BerryBlendz.
  3. On its berry-gradient world page, hit ▶ PLAY.
  4. The candy-gradient ◀ BACK TO TOKYUBE button (top-left) appears on the game's title screen and returns you to the VoxelVerse — no splash replay, scaled to your Options display resolution.

How It Plays

  • ◀ ▶ aim your drop, SPACE lets the berry fall (mouse and touch work too).
  • Same berries merge on contact into the next size up — plan your stack or drown in fruit.
  • The shop and the scoreboards keep the runs coming.

Pausing

BerryBlendz pauses natively with ESC or P (and auto-pauses when the window loses focus); the HUD has a ⏸ button too. Resume with the same keys or SPACE.

The Berry World Page

The discovery page pours the whole berry spectrum: deep blue → indigo → purple → hot pink → red, with a white-bordered candy PLAY button and glacier-blue accents. Likes, comments and visits work like every other world page.

Status
